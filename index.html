03 — What we believe

Six principles
we won't break.

The promises we keep — across every engagement, regardless of scope. Written here, written into every contract.

Komitmen yang tidak kami negosiasi — pada setiap project, berapapun ukurannya.

P.01
Pixel-perfect, never "close enough".

Designs that match the spec down to the last padding value. The detail you notice is the detail your visitors feel.

P.02
Mobile is the first draft, not the afterthought.

Most of your visitors are on a phone. Designed for the small screen first, scaled up gracefully.

P.03
Performance is a feature, not a polish.

Lighthouse 90+ across the board. Fast sites convert. Slow sites bleed visitors before they read a word.

P.04
SEO from line one of the foundation.

Semantic HTML, heading hierarchy, schema markup — engineered in from the start, not bolted on at launch.

P.05
Daily updates. Never ghost. No jargon.

Progress shared every working day. Plain language. You'll always know where we stand and what's next.

P.06
Delivered when promised — or you're refunded.

If we commit to Friday, it ships Friday. If we miss, you receive 10% off per day late. Written into every contract.
